3. IRIS PAYMENT PROCESSING FEE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Strategy A — Platform collects subscription via IRIS (free for business owner)
    • Customer sees IBAN, pays via mobile banking app
    • IRIS is FREE for the payer in Greece (no bank fees)
    • Reduces card processing costs vs Stripe (Stripe = 1.4% + 0.25€)

  Strategy B — Charge per IRIS transaction generated for customers
    • €0.10–0.20 per IRIS payment link/request created
    • Bill monthly based on count
    • Fishermen issue 50–200 invoices/month → €5–€40/month extra revenue

  Strategy C — IRIS Premium Feature
    • Only in Professional+ plans
    • Basic plan: manual IBAN display only
    • Pro+: Automated IRIS request, status tracking, PDF with QR code

  IRIS growth: Greece's IRIS usage grew 400% in 2023-2025 as banks promoted it.
  All major Greek banks support it: NBG, Eurobank, Alpha, Piraeus, Attica, etc.

  FishBill integrates with licensed myDATA providers (SoftOne, EpsilonNet,
  UniDoc, Entersoft, Singular Logic, Megasoft). These companies have reseller /
  affiliate programs.

  How to get paid:
  1. Negotiate reseller agreements with each provider
  2. When a user selects a provider in FishBill settings, show a referral link
  3. Earn commission per activated account referred

  Typical commissions (estimate):
    • SoftOne:      5–15% of first-year license (~€150–500/customer/year)
    • EpsilonNet:   10–20% referral bonus (~€100–300/customer)
    • UniDoc:       Per-transmission fee share (~€0.05–0.15/transmission)

  With 500 customers each sending ~150 invoices/month via provider:
  500 × 150 × 12 × €0.08 commission = €72,000/year passive.

5. ACCOUNTANT RESELLER / WHITE-LABEL PROGRAM

  Greek accountants (λογιστές) handle the books for most fishing businesses.
  They are the gatekeepers to acquiring fishermen as customers.

  Model A — Accountant Bulk License
    • Accountant pays €50/month, manages up to 20 clients at €3/client
    • They resell at €10–15/client → margin of €7–12/client/month
    • 50 accountants × 20 clients × €50 = €50,000 MRR from accountant channel

  Model B — White-Label for Accounting Firms
    • Large accounting offices get custom-branded version
    • "Γιαννόπουλος Λογιστική — Powered by FishBill"
    • License fee: €200–500/month per firm
    • They handle support, you handle product

  Model C — Referral Commission
    • Accountant earns €5/month per active client they bring
    • No upfront cost, purely performance-based
    • Aligns accountant incentive with retention

  Greece has ~45,000 registered accountants (ΟΕΕ members). Even 0.1% in the
  fisheries niche = 45 accountant partners managing ~900+ fishing businesses.

6. SMS / EMAIL PLATFORM MARKUP

  Instead of letting users configure their own provider keys, offer a managed
  "FishBill Messaging" service:

  • FishBill has a master account on Apifon / Routee / Brevo
  • Includes SMS credits in plan pricing (e.g., 50 SMS/month in Pro plan)
  • Overage at €0.05/SMS (Routee bulk rate ≈ €0.008–0.015/SMS → 3–5x markup)
  • Email: 500 included, €0.002/email overage (Brevo costs ~€0.0003/email)

  This eliminates the user's need to sign up for external providers — simpler UX,
  higher LTV, and a meaningful margin on messaging volume.

  Phase 2 feature: FishBill connects fishermen (supply) with buyers (demand).

  • Fisherman lists catch as available ("σήμερα ψαρεύω 300kg λαβράκι")
  • Restaurants, fish markets, wholesalers browse and order
  • FishBill earns 2–5% transaction fee on each sale
  • Invoice is auto-generated on order confirmation

  Why it works:
    • Already have both sides: fishermen (suppliers) and their customers
    • The invoice data shows who buys what — existing buyer relationships
    • ΑΑΔΕ already requires invoicing this flow — FishBill removes friction

  Revenue projection: if 100 fishermen each transact €5,000/month through
  the marketplace at 3% fee = €15,000/month from marketplace alone.
